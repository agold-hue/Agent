# Life events playbook

A life event is a project (projects/<slug>.md) run over weeks: restate the dates and constraints, write the checklist below into the file with an owner and a status per line, start what can start (start_task, one per item), set follow-ups for the rest, and report progress in the morning brief. Ask the user only for what is in their head: dates, choices, documents you cannot get.

## Moving
- Utilities: stop at the old address, start at the new (electric, gas, water, internet, trash); dates a day either side of the move. Final bills tracked.
- Address changes: USPS forwarding, bank and cards, insurance, DMV, employer, subscriptions, voter registration, doctor and dentist, Amazon and delivery apps, school.
- Movers or truck: three quotes, insurance, elevator and parking bookings with both buildings.
- Renters or homeowners insurance effective on the new date; lease or closing documents filed to Drive.
- Keys, locks, alarm codes; cleaning of the old place; deposit return chased at 30 days.

## New baby
- Insurance: add the child within 30 days; pediatrician chosen and first visit booked; birth certificate and Social Security number tracked.
- Leave paperwork with the employer; FSA or dependent-care changes; will and beneficiaries updated (checkpoint before anything is changed).
- Registry, deliveries timed to the due date; recurring orders (diapers, formula) set up as standing orders.

## Buying or leasing a car
- Budget and financing pre-approval; three quotes on the same trim; trade-in value; insurance quote before signing.
- Registration, inspection and insurance dates into renewals.md with follow-ups; the loan or lease into renewals.md; a car pickup is an appointment item.

## Trip abroad
- Passport validity (six months past return), visas or ETAs, vaccinations; travel notices to banks; phone plan or eSIM.
- Bookings end to end: flights, lodging, transfers, key reservations; every confirmation as a reservation item and on calendar.md; check-in follow-ups the day before each leg; a packing and documents list to Drive.
- Home while away: deliveries paused or rerouted, plants and pets arranged, mail held.
