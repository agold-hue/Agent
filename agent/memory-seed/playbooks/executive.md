# Executive playbook (a CEO or founder)

Data: contacts.md (Work: team, board, investors, customers), actions.md, calendar, owner_inbox, board.md (cadence, asks), metrics forwarded weekly (observation lane), topics.md.

## Calendar defense
- Rules in standing_instructions.md: focus blocks, meeting cap per day, no-meeting mornings, who always gets time, what needs an agenda. Enforce them when scheduling (calendar playbook). Decline drafts are short and offer an alternative.
- Weekly: propose what to cut, batch, or delegate from next week's calendar.

## Inbox with delegation
- Inbox playbook, plus: route by owner. Anything that belongs to a team member (contacts.md Work) gets a forward draft with a one-line ask and a date, a row in actions.md, and a chase. The user sees only "decide" items and a count of what you routed.

## Action items across the company
- Every commitment to or from the user, from mail and meeting notes the user forwards or dictates, lands in actions.md with owner and date. Chase owners two days after due by draft or `send_email` (auto-approved for internal addresses if the user allows "message" for the company domain). The brief shows only what is slipping.

## Board and investors
- board.md: meeting dates, deck deadline, who owes inputs, standing asks from each board member. Four weeks before a board meeting: start the project, request inputs from each owner, chase, assemble the outline. Two weeks before: draft ready for the user.
- Monthly investor update: from the forwarded metrics report plus highlights the user gives you, drafted in their voice from the last update's structure; the user approves; you send via `send_email` or leave a draft.
- Every ask a board member or investor makes is an action item with them as requester.

## Relationships
- People playbook, with investor and key-customer cadences. Before any meeting with them: brief with last contact, what they asked, what was promised.

## Contracts and renewals
- Every vendor contract, SaaS renewal, lease, and policy in renewals.md with notice window and price. Start renegotiation at the start of the notice window: three quotes or a benchmark, a recommendation, and the notice letter drafted. Auto-renewals flagged two weeks ahead.

## Hiring pipeline
- ATS notifications forwarded in. Schedule interviews across panel calendars (calendar tool for the user; email for others), send candidates prep notes in the user's voice, collect scorecards (chase missing ones after 24h), summarize the panel for the decision, and draft the offer or the decline. Offers are agreements: checkpoint.

## Signals
- Research playbook digest, with the company, competitors, customers, hiring market, regulation, and press mentions. Negative mentions are URGENT.
