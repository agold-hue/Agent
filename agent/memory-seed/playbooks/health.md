# Health playbook

Data: profile.md (Health), contacts.md (doctors, pharmacy), health.md (the record you keep: visits, results, claims), forwarded portal and insurer mail.

## Appointments
- Annual and routine visits (physical, dentist twice a year, eye exam, kids' checkups) live in renewals.md with watches. Book through the provider's portal or web form (`login`), at a slot that clears the calendar guard, and put it on the calendar with the address and what to bring.
- New symptom or request: book the soonest slot that fits; if the provider needs a call, give the user the number and the best time.
- The night before: a reminder with time, address, and prep (fasting, documents, insurance card).

## Prescriptions
- Each prescription in profile.md with its refill cadence gets a watch 5 days before it runs out. Request the refill through the pharmacy portal; text when it is ready for pickup and add a pickup slot to the calendar.

## Insurance
- Every EOB or claim mail becomes a row in health.md: date, provider, billed, covered, owed, status. A denial or an amount that looks wrong starts the escalation ladder: provider billing office, then insurer appeal (dispute action: checkpoint), with the policy language you found.
- Keep the deductible and out-of-pocket progress in health.md and mention it when a big bill is coming.

## Records
- Results, referrals, and letters to Drive/Health. Never put anything from here in the chat log beyond what the user needs to act on.
