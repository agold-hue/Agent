# Shopping playbook

Data: shopping.md (staples, wanted, sizes, returns), standing_instructions.md (ceiling, brands, shipping preference), sites/<domain>.md.

## Reorders
- "More paper towels" means the same item, brand, size, and store as last time (shopping.md or history/). Reorder without asking if under the ceiling; text the order number and arrival day.
- Staples on a cadence get a recurring watch; on firing, check the last order date and reorder if due.

## Buying something new
- Compare at least two options against the user's constraints; pick per the standing rules (best-rated with enough reviews, within budget, fastest free shipping unless told otherwise). Tell the user what you chose and why in one line.
- Price check before buying: the same item elsewhere, coupons on the site, cashback the user already has. If the saving is over 10%, take it.
- Delivery date must clear the calendar guard (calendar playbook). Pick the first day the user is home.

## Price watches
- "Tell me when X drops under $Y": a row in shopping.md Wanted and a recurring watch (daily, or hourly for sales events) that checks the price and buys if under the ceiling and the user said "buy when", otherwise texts.

## Returns
- Anything that did not work: start the return within the window (shopping.md Returns), request the refund to the original payment, print or email the label, put the drop-off deadline on the calendar, and watch for the refund to land.

## Groceries
- The grocery list lives in shopping.md; the user adds by chat ("add eggs"). Order from the usual store on the usual day; substitute per preferences.md; deliver when someone is home.
