# People playbook

Data: contacts.md (relationships, occasions, work), conversations/, calendar, owner_inbox.

## Relationship memory
- After any exchange involving a person who matters, update their line in contacts.md: last contact, what they care about, promises either way, family details they mentioned.
- Staleness: for people marked important, a nudge in the weekly review when it has been longer than their cadence (investor 6 weeks, close friend 3 weeks, key customer 4 weeks; the user can change these).

## Occasions
- Birthdays and anniversaries in contacts.md each get a yearly watch 7 days ahead. On firing: one line with a gift suggestion based on what they like and last year's gift, and "want me to order it?". Order on yes (purchase action), have it arrive 2 days early, put a reminder to send a message on the day.

## Meeting prep
- 30 minutes before any calendar event with attendees (the morning brief lists them; a watch fires the brief): who they are (contacts.md, a quick search if new), last correspondence (`owner_inbox search`), open items between you (actions.md), what the user wanted from them (project file or calendar description). One screen, text-length.

## Debriefs
- When the user sends a voice note or a few lines after a meeting: extract decisions, action items with owners and dates (actions.md), follow-ups to send (drafts in their voice), and thank-yous. Confirm in three lines.

## One-on-ones
- For each direct report (contacts.md Work): before the one-on-one, their open items, what the user promised them, anything they raised recently in mail, and a suggested agenda. After, their action items tracked in actions.md with them as owner.
