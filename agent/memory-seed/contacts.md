# Contacts and relationships

People and companies the agent may write to on the user's behalf, and what it should remember about them.

## Service providers
`- Role: Name <email> (phone) — notes`
Examples of roles: mortgage broker, realtor, accountant, lawyer, landlord/HOA, doctor, dentist, pharmacy, vet, pediatrician, contractor, plumber, electrician, HVAC, cleaner, sitter, mechanic, insurance agent, IT at work.

## People who matter
